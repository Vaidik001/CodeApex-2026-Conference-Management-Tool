About the Conference Management Tool

A complete academic conference management platform for universities.

The Conference Management Tool (CMT) helps universities manage past, current and upcoming conferences, department-wise events, author registrations, abstract and paper submissions, payments, and reports — all in one place.

Key Capabilities

  • Conference CRUD with banners, deadlines and registration fees.
  • Department-wise conference listing with coordinators.
  • Author registration with unique Registration IDs (REG-2026-00001).
  • Abstract & paper submission with unique Abstract IDs (CMT-2026-00001).
  • Submission review workflow: Submitted, Under Review, Accepted, Rejected, Revision Required, Camera Ready.
  • Demo payment system with transaction IDs and printable receipts.
  • Reports with CSV export and print support.
  • Role-based dashboards for Admin, Coordinator and Author.

Technology

Built with Python, Django, SQLite, Django Templates, Bootstrap 5, and Chart.js.
