## 💡 Project Overview

The objective of this project was to develop a centralized **Conference Management Tool (CMT)** for managing the complete conference lifecycle.

The system is designed to help organizers manage conferences, authors, paper submissions, registrations, presentations and reports through a single platform.

---

## ✨ Key Features

- 📅 Past, Current & Upcoming Conference Management
- 🏢 Department-wise Conference Organization
- 📄 Conference Details & Promotional Materials
- 👤 Author Registration
- 📝 Paper Submission
- 🔢 Unique Abstract ID Generation
- 💳 Conference Registration & Payment
- 🔍 Submission Search & Status Tracking
- 🎤 Paper & Poster Presentation Management
- 📊 Conference & Submission Reports
